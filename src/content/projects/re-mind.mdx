An agentic chatbot that helped resurface ideas, goals, and intentions to encourage meaningful follow-through, guided by cognitive and behavioral research.

## More info

re-mind was a way to take back control of what pops up in your mind — determining ***when***, ***how*** and ***how often*** to resurface ideas, goals, and intentions to nudge you to act on them.
Built with [Nicole](https://linkedin.com/in/nicole-kezlik/) using PydanticAI, FastAPI, Celery, Redis, and PostgreSQL, deployed as a chatbot for Telegram and WhatsApp.
We wound it down in 2026 — it would have struggled to stand out in a crowded space while solving too narrow a problem, so we chose to put the energy elsewhere.
