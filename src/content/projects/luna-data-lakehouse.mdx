Data lakehouse for rider assistance R&D, giving the team a single organized, queryable home for its video and sensor data from motorcycles and bikes.

## More info

Designed the architecture with scenario tagging built in - weather, lighting and more - so every recording carries the conditions it was captured in.
Plugged into evaluation, **model performance could be broken down by condition**, showing where it holds up and where it falls short.
