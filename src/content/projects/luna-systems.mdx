Technical advisory for Luna Systems, a startup building AI-based rider assistance systems for motorcycles and bikes. Brought in to identify the biggest gaps across research, engineering and product, and close them.

## More info

**Evaluation:** designed and built an evaluation framework structured around the product's safety functions rather than raw model scores, storing model outputs so metrics can be recomputed as definitions evolve. **Moved R&D toward metrics-driven development.**
**Data:** designed a data lakehouse to give the team one organized, queryable home for its video and sensor data, with scenario tagging (weather, lighting and more) so model performance could be broken down by condition.
**Product:** defined the function portfolio and customer-facing technical specifications, with emphasis on empirical definitions of success and measurable KPIs.
**Customers:** sharpened positioning and proposals, and served as the technical counterpart in meetings with tier 1s and OEMs, including an on-site workshop in Japan.
